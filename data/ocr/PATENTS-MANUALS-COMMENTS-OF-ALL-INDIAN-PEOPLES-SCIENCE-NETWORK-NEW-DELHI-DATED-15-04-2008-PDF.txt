. The language of Section 3 k) of the The Patents Act, 1970 makes it clear that

. The clause that software per se 15 not patentable' would mean that only

application is 8 part of a larger system and the system as a whole is eligible for
a patent, can the invention be patented as a whole. This is the intent of the Act
and therefore we are sure that the Patent office would take this into cognizance
when deciding on patent claims.
